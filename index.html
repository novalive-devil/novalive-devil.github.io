/ QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

Need something else? The portal is designed to grow as you add your own resources.

Is NOVALIVE free to use?＋

Yes. The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, and the included browser tools run on your device. Some external downloads or services may have their own terms.

How do I download a resource?＋

Open the portal, find a resource card and click its download link. At the moment, the sample files are simple text guides; your own files and links can be added later.

How do I add my social links?＋

Instagram, YouTube, Telegram and WhatsApp are connected. Click any social card to open the corresponding profile or channel.

Can I add my own tools and files?＋

Yes. You can upload static files to the repository and update the resource cards. A web-based admin upload dashboard would need a separate backend or storage service.
