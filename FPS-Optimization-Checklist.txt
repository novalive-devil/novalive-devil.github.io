NOVALIVE — PC GAMING PERFORMANCE CHECKLIST
===========================================

Use this as a checklist, not a promise of extra FPS. Results depend on your hardware and game.

BEFORE CHANGING SETTINGS
[ ] Write down your current graphics and display settings.
[ ] Update Windows through its normal update settings.
[ ] Install GPU drivers from the official NVIDIA, AMD or Intel website/app.
[ ] Keep enough free storage for Windows and the game to update properly.
[ ] Close apps and browser tabs you do not need while gaming.

IN-GAME SETTINGS
[ ] Choose the correct monitor and refresh rate.
[ ] Test fullscreen or borderless mode and keep whichever performs best for your setup.
[ ] Lower demanding settings one at a time (shadows, reflections, volumetrics).
[ ] Use an upscaling option only if your GPU/game supports it and the image looks acceptable.
[ ] Set an FPS cap if it improves frame pacing or temperature for your setup.
[ ] Check whether V-Sync, VRR/G-SYNC/FreeSync settings suit your monitor.

STABILITY AND SAFETY
[ ] Check temperatures and clock behaviour with a trusted monitoring tool.
[ ] Keep laptop vents clear; do not block cooling intakes.
[ ] Avoid unknown "FPS booster" executables, registry packs and driver download sites.
[ ] Do not disable security tools or essential Windows services based on random guides.
[ ] Change one setting at a time, test a repeatable scene, and revert if worse.
